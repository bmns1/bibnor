What we build

FOUR PLACES WE USUALLY START

Most schools already have the data. It is just spread across a dozen spreadsheets, three systems and one person who remembers how it all fits together. We build the system that holds it in one place — and put AI on the parts nobody enjoys doing by hand.

Admissions & enrollment

Applications, waitlists and offers that follow your school's calendar and your criteria — not a vendor's.

Attendance & timetabling

Rosters and schedules that stay right on their own, and flag the clash before it reaches a classroom.

Reporting & insight

AI reads what the school already collects and writes the report — attendance dips, at-risk students, board packs.

Integrations

Talks to the SIS, LMS and finance tools you already pay for. No rip-and-replace, no data held hostage!

How we work

THREE STAGES, IN ORDER
01 — MAP

We learn your school

We sit with the people who run it and map how things actually happen today: the spreadsheets, the workarounds, the bits nobody has had time to fix.

02 — BUILD

We build around that

The system is shaped to the map, not to a template. AI takes the repetitive weight — data cleanup, first-draft reports, timetable conflicts, routine parent comms.

03 — RUN

We stay through the year

Support, changes and new modules as the school year moves. You keep talking to the people who built it — no ticket queue in between.

What you can count on

THE FINE PRINT, UP FRONT

Your data stays yours

Everything lives in open, exportable formats. If we ever part ways, you leave with your records complete, clean and usable — no lock-in, no export fee.

Privacy by default

Student records are sensitive. Access is role-based and audited, and AI features run on the minimum data they need — never on records they have no reason to see.

AI where it earns its place

AI does the repetitive work — cleanup, first drafts, flags worth a look. Decisions that affect a student stay with the people responsible for them.
